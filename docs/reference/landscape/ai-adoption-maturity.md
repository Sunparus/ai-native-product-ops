# Landscape — AI Adoption & Organizational Maturity

## Open Questions

1. **Why maturity stalls** — MIT CISR's research found roughly half of organizations still sit in the two earliest maturity stages as of the most recent survey, down from a larger share previously — what specifically separates the organizations that break through from the ones that stay stuck in funded pilots that never scale?

## Trends

1. **Financial performance tracks maturity stage** — MIT CISR research found organizations in the top two maturity stages perform above their industry's financial average; organizations in the bottom two stages perform below it. Maturity isn't just an operational nicety — it correlates with outcomes.
