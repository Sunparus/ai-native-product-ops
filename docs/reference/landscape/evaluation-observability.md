# Landscape — Evaluation & Observability

## Open Questions

## Trends

1. **System-level evaluation maturing into a production discipline** — Evaluation moving from academic benchmarks into LLMOps: CI-integrated eval pipelines, not just pre-launch checklists.

| Who | Why them |
