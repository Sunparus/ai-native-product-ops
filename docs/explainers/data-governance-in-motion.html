    Ask in the room

    Five questions that show whether governance runs in daily work or only on paper. The answers are illustrative and show what each kind of answer sounds like.

    Who approves an AI use case reusing this dataset?

    Rubber stamp"Data governance handles that."
    Real answer"The domain's data owner signs off per use case after a purpose-compatibility check. The privacy team is consulted, and the approval is logged against the dataset."

    How does a sensitivity tag change what the AI system does?

    Rubber stamp"We classify all our data."
    Real answer"Router, retrieval and tool calls all ask one policy engine that reads the tags. Untagged data is denied by default, and we test that in CI."

    Do access rights survive into the vector index?

    Rubber stamp"The index is internal, so it's fine."
    Real answer"Each chunk inherits its source's classification and access list. Retrieval filters by the asker's groups before ranking."

    Can we explain one specific AI decision six months later?

    Rubber stamp"We have lineage in the catalog."
    Real answer"Yes. Column-level lineage up to retrieval, plus a per-response trace of retrieved chunks, model version and prompt version."

    Which business outcome or risk is this governance program tied to?

    Rubber stamp"Good data hygiene."
    Real answer"Getting the eligibility model approved for launch. We track owner coverage, the share of its inputs classified, and the age of open quality alerts."

    Legal anchors (EU)

    Where the scenes meet binding law. This is orientation, not legal advice.

    AnchorWhat it requiresApplies
    GDPR Art. 5Principles for personal data, including purpose limitation (no incompatible further use, test in Art. 6(4)), data minimisation and storage limitation.In force
    GDPR Art. 22Limits on solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. The CJEU held that a score can itself be such a decision where it plays a determining role (C-634/21, 2023), and that people are owed an explanation of the procedure and principles actually applied (C-203/22, 2025).In force
    AI Act Art. 10, 12, 13For high-risk AI (Annex III, e.g. credit, hiring, essential services): governance of training, validation and test data, including bias examination, plus logging and transparency to deployers.Annex III from 2 Dec 2027 (Digital Omnibus)
    GDPR Chapter VTransfers outside the EU need an adequacy decision or safeguards such as SCCs. The EU–US Data Privacy Framework was upheld by the General Court (T-553/23, 3 Sep 2025); an appeal is pending at the CJEU.In force
